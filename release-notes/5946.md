### Full release notes (wiki)
<!-- rn:full:start -->
**LinqToDB — ⚠ Breaking changes**
- Reading an entity that has dynamic columns but nowhere to store them — no `[DynamicColumnsStore]` member and no setter configured through `[DynamicColumnAccessor]` or `DynamicPropertyAccessors` — now throws `InvalidOperationException` naming the type and the columns. Previously the dynamic values were silently dropped, and with a `Storage`-backed association loaded through `LoadWith` the query failed with a `NullReferenceException`. Add a store member or a setter, or project the columns you need explicitly.
<!-- rn:full:end -->
