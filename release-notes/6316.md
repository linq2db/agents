### Full release notes (wiki)
<!-- rn:full:start -->
**LinqToDB — Added**
- .NET 11's tuple-returning `Join`, `LeftJoin`, `RightJoin` and `FullJoin` overloads (no result selector) are translated, including further composition over the tuple, e.g. `db.Parent.Join(db.Child, p => p.ParentID, c => c.ParentID).Where(t => t.Inner.ChildID > 20)`.
- The `comparer` argument of `Join`, `LeftJoin`, `RightJoin` and `FullJoin` is accepted when it is `null`, and the query translates as if no comparer were passed. A non-null comparer still can't be translated to SQL.
<!-- rn:full:end -->
