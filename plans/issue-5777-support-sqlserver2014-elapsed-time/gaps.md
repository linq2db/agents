## Aggregate

19 of 20 findings trace to blocks a Tier M plan requires (`P4` × 8, `P7` × 7, `P8` × 2, `P9` × 2). The twentieth (MIN006) needs a corpus rule that does not exist yet, and since the branch is Tier L, the critic pass would also have been mandatory. Sometimes that was the storage types and versions flowing into a new or un-gated lowering (MAJ001, MAJ004, MIN001, MIN002, MIN003, MIN007), sometimes the other readers and encodings of the flipped capability (MAJ003, MIN008, NIT002). The single change that prevents the most findings (9) is widening the scout brief's producer sweep from defect fixes to capability-enabling changes, paired with a sweep of everything that reads the flipped capability.
