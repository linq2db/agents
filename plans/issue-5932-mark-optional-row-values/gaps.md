### Aggregate

The classes are spread out (GAP-05 ×3, GAP-09 ×2, GAP-02 ×1, GAP-03 ×1), but five of the seven findings (MIN001, MIN002, MIN005, MIN006, SUG001) share one mechanism. An amendment changed a rule (A-11 and A-15 on the clamp tables, A-17 on the node, A-19 on the count rule) without re-deriving the obligations and claims that rested on the old rule. Concretely: TO-7f's cells followed the clamp table instead of the lowering predicate, YDB's coordinate was never re-read for the new arms, TO-7e was left vacuous, and TO-8 stayed SQLite-only after a change SQLite cannot render. A-18 onward also ran critic-waived. The other two findings come from an unreconciled tail: four commits after the plan's last update, three unauthorized source files, and `-Reconcile` never run. The single change that would have prevented the most findings: require every P11 amendment to name each SC, U, TO and gate scope whose premise it changes, and to re-derive them, even when the critic is waived.
