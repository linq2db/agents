## Aggregate

The findings spread across three classes: GAP-02 ×4, GAP-05 ×3, GAP-01 ×3. They share one theme: the harness's own self-checks were each proven red on only one of their clauses or axes. TO-2's SQL clause, TO-8's gated-row and wrong-reason arms, and TO-11's client-library axis were all untested, so the plan's guarantees were true for one arm and asserted for the rest. The single most effective change is a `P8` rule that every assertive clause of a `TO-n`, and every axis the row key does not separate, owes its own mutation recorded in `G-01`. It would have prevented MIN002 and SUG001 outright and caught MIN001 and MIN003 through TO-8's missing arms. The other misses are premises made stale or never extended by later edits: A-1's stdout switch, round 2's D-6 rewrite and G-10x's timeout re-set each changed a fact without re-checking the rows that relied on it.
