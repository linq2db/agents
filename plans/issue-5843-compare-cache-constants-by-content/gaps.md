## Aggregate

Both attributed findings are GAP-02, and both have the same shape. The plan checked one side of a two-sided mechanism and assumed the other. For R2-1, the checked side was what `BuildConstant` registers and the assumed side was what node reaches it. For R2-2, the checked side was that compiled queries build the compares and the assumed side was that something reads them. The repo already has rules for both sides (`work-plan.md:86` write-vs-read, `work-plan.md:90` list the producers upstream), but both are scoped to `P4` rows, and this plan carried both claims elsewhere (a P7 row, an amendment's mechanism text). The one change that would have prevented both: make those two rules apply to any checkable mechanism claim, wherever in the plan it sits. R2-3 was refuted by measurement and is not attributed. Every P9 row passes, and no gate was applicable to either finding, so there is no GAP-08.
