## Pattern matched
Cross-provider inconsistency: siblings that detect the same condition (an insert that needs the generated key) use two different predicates. Whether `NeedsIdentity` covers InsertOrUpdate or other statement kinds that `Insert.WithIdentity` on a plain `SqlInsertStatement` does not is not visible from the services.

## Why it matters
Providers can diverge silently on which statement kinds trigger identity retrieval, and a new provider author has no single idiom to copy.

## Suggested fix
Pick one predicate (likely `NeedsIdentity`, on the base class as a protected helper) and use it everywhere, or document why DB2, Informix, Firebird and Oracle need the narrower form.
